## Methodology

The nutritional information for this recipe was calculated using the following methodology:

1. **Data Collection**: Nutritional values for each ingredient were sourced from reputable databases and standardized to values per 100g.

2. **Quantity Conversion**: The recipe quantities were converted to grams for precise calculation.

3. **Nutritional Calculation**: For each ingredient, the nutritional values were calculated proportionally based on the amount used in the recipe:
   ```
   Nutrient value in recipe = (Amount in recipe ÷ 100) × Nutrient value per 100g
   ```

4. **Total Recipe Values**: The nutritional values for all ingredients were summed to determine the total nutritional content of the entire recipe.

5. **Per Cookie Values**: The total nutritional values were divided by the recipe yield (9 cookies) to determine the nutritional content per cookie.

### Notes:

- The recipe states it yields 8-10 cookies; calculations used 9 cookies as the average yield.
- Micronutrient values (sodium, potassium, calcium, iron) were included where reliable data was available.
- The nutritional profile reflects the recipe as written, including the chocolate glaze and raspberry garnish.
- Values are rounded to the nearest decimal place for practical use.

This nutritional analysis provides a comprehensive overview of the Crumbl Kourtney's Flourless Chocolate Cake Cookie, highlighting its rich chocolate flavor profile reflected in the high fat and moderate carbohydrate content, with notable amounts of fiber from the dark chocolate and cocoa powder.
