# Crumbl Kourtney's Flourless Chocolate Cake Cookie: Copycat Recipe

*Note: This recipe is a copycat version based on available information about Crumbl's Kourtney's Flourless Chocolate Cake Cookie. It is not the official recipe but aims to recreate the key characteristics: flourless chocolate base, natural sweeteners (honey and applesauce), dark chocolate glaze, and raspberry garnish.*

## Ingredients

### For the Flourless Chocolate Cookie Base:
- 8 oz (225g) high-quality dark chocolate (70% cocoa), chopped
- 1/2 cup (115g) unsalted butter
- 3 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1/4 cup (85g) honey
- 1/4 cup (60g) unsweetened applesauce
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup (35g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 tsp baking powder (gluten-free if desired)

### For the Dark Chocolate Glaze:
- 4 oz (115g) dark chocolate (70% cocoa), chopped
- 1/4 cup (60ml) heavy cream
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter

### For Garnish:
- Fresh raspberries (3 per cookie)

## Instructions

### For the Flourless Chocolate Cookie Base:

1. **Prepare**: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. **Melt Chocolate**: In a heatproof bowl over simmering water (double boiler), melt the dark chocolate and butter together, stirring occasionally until smooth. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.

3. **Mix Wet Ingredients**: In a large bowl, whisk together eggs, honey, applesauce, and vanilla extract until well combined.

4. **Combine**: Slowly pour the melted chocolate mixture into the egg mixture, whisking continuously to prevent the eggs from cooking.

5. **Add Dry Ingredients**: Sift the cocoa powder, salt, and baking powder over the chocolate mixture. Fold gently until just combined.

6. **Form Cookies**: Using an ice cream scoop or 1/4 cup measure, scoop the batter onto the prepared baking sheet, leaving about 2 inches between each cookie. The batter will be quite soft, more like cake batter than traditional cookie dough.

7. **Bake**: Bake for 11-13 minutes until the edges are set but the center is still slightly soft. The cookies will rise slightly and may crack on top.

8. **Cool**: Allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

### For the Dark Chocolate Glaze:

1. **Heat Cream**: In a small saucepan, heat the heavy cream until it just begins to simmer.

2. **Make Glaze**: Place chopped chocolate in a heatproof bowl. Pour the hot cream over the chocolate and let sit for 1 minute. Add honey and butter, then stir until smooth and glossy.

3. **Cool Slightly**: Allow the glaze to cool for about 5 minutes until it thickens slightly but is still pourable.

### Assembly:

1. **Glaze Cookies**: Spoon the chocolate glaze over each cooled cookie, allowing it to drip down the sides.

2. **Garnish**: Place three fresh raspberries on top of each cookie.

3. **Serve**: For the authentic Crumbl experience, warm the cookies slightly before serving.

## Notes:
- This recipe yields approximately 8-10 cookies, depending on size.
- The cookies are best enjoyed within 2 days of baking.
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature.
- For a truly gluten-free version, ensure all ingredients are certified gluten-free.
- The texture should be somewhere between a brownie and a cake - moist with a slightly crisp exterior.
