# Crumbl Kourtney's Flourless Chocolate Cake Cookie: Comprehensive Description

## Overview
The Kourtney's Flourless Chocolate Cake Cookie is a specialty item from Crumbl Cookies, created in collaboration with Kourtney Kardashian. This unique dessert is positioned as a healthier alternative to traditional cookies, featuring no refined sugar and a gluten-friendly formulation. Priced at $6.98 individually, it represents Crumbl's venture into more health-conscious dessert options.

## Appearance
- Dark chocolate cake-like cookie base
- Topped with a velvety dark chocolate glaze
- Garnished with three fresh raspberries
- Served warm
- Resembles a small chocolate cake more than a traditional cookie
